\section{The ledger of the wordnets}\label{app:inventory}
\cref{tab:inventory} lists the type-level ledger of \cref{sec:inventory} for every wordnet of the Open Multilingual Wordnet package with at least $1{,}000$ pairs, and for English WordNet~3.0. A dagger marks wordnets with fewer than $10{,}000$ synsets, which are not in \cref{fig:inventory}. Each distinct (synset, lemma) pair is counted once; $\Lat$ and $\Dbt$ are in bits. The resources differ in how they were built, so the rows are comparable as descriptions of the resources and not as measurements of languages.
