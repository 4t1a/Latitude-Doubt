\section{Limits and open problems}\label{sec:limits}
\paragraph{A partition is not all of meaning.} The ledger accounts for a chosen $W$ or a chosen $Y$. It does not say that either is what a speaker intends. \cref{thm:leak}(d) is a proof of this limitation, not a way around it: every meaning in the theory is meaning for something. Meaning as intention, as truth-conditions or as reference is outside the scope.

\paragraph{Meaning-preserving is relative to the map.} The embedding of \cref{sec:embedding} preserves the amino-acid sequence, which is the meaning map of this paper. It does not preserve everything biology cares about, and synonymous changes can affect expression, folding and function \citep{plotkin2011,kimchi2007}. A purpose that includes these has a finer meaning and a smaller latitude (\cref{thm:derived}(d)), and the capacity of the channel is that smaller latitude. The theorem is exact for the cover law it is given; choosing a cover law that is acceptable to the host is outside it.

\paragraph{Independence.} The coding theorems assume independent draws. Text is not independent, and neither are the codons of a gene. The ledger identities hold for any joint law, including a joint law over blocks or over stationary processes with entropy rates; the coding statements would need the corresponding theorems for that setting. The estimates in \cref{sec:measurements} treat genes (or documents) as the independent unit and codons (or tokens) within them as exchangeable draws from the class law, which is an approximation.

\paragraph{What the genome measurements show.} The shares of relevance carried by synonymous choice describe six chosen genomes and two purposes; other panels would give other numbers. That identity sits in the latitude is consistent with the known dependence of synonymous codon choice on base composition \citep{sueoka1962,muto1987} and is expected. The plastid, with $85$ genes, is a small sample. The held-out test supports the decomposition on these genomes; it does not make the shares universal.

\paragraph{What the English measurements show.} The sign of the balance $E-C=\Lat-\Dbt$ is not a property of English as such. In SemCor it is positive for lemmas, becomes negative when inflected words are the forms, is near zero for adjectives, and in wordnet inventories has no fixed sign (\cref{sec:semcor,sec:inventory}). The meaning is a WordNet synset, a fine-grained inventory on which annotators themselves disagree; the corpus is American English of the 1960s; the whole-corpus values have not converged; and only the well-attested core, which is a restriction of the source, is stable. We tested one corpus and one family of inventories. A second sense-tagged corpus, in particular of another genre or language, would be the natural next test and was not available to us.

\paragraph{Leakage depends on the model.} Leakage is estimated from data through models of $P(y\given x)$ and $P(y\given w)$, and a poor model can overstate or understate it. On the genomes we computed it from tabulated codon laws with samples of $10^5$ codons or more, and checked it against held-out genes. A study of leakage in language would need a design that respects the dependence within documents, which we did not attempt.

\paragraph{Open problems.}
(1) An extension of \cref{thm:channel} to sources with memory and to receivers who observe the text through a noisy channel; latitude would then compete with error correction for the same freedom.
(2) A network version of the ledger, in which several speakers share meanings and the doubt of one is the latitude of another.
(3) A calibrated estimator of the leakage of a declared meaning under dependence, with a guarantee.
(4) A test of the vocabulary law against languages with known lexicons, where the effective vocabulary $2^E$ can be compared with the size of the lexicon.

\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}
Meaning was set aside in 1948 because the engineering problem did not need it. The ledger takes it back with no new mathematics: $E=I+\Lat$ and $C=I+\Dbt$. What is said differs from what is meant by the freedom of the speaker minus the doubt of the listener. Latitude costs vocabulary and, in an idealized model, buys a channel; doubt costs questions. Which meaning one uses is a choice of purpose, and the leakage says how much that choice discards. On the genetic code the numbers are definite: about a quarter of the entropy of a codon is latitude, and in the genomes measured nearly nine tenths of what a codon says about where it came from is written there. In English the balance between latitude and doubt changes sign with the definition of the form, which is a reason to state that definition whenever the ledger is reported.
